HOW TO PLAY 1) Without SLOW/FAST you only walk the white borders. 2) HOLD SLOW (Z) or FAST (X), then HOLD arrows into the dark area to draw a line. 3) Keep holding until the line touches a border again — that loop fills and reveals the image. 4) Repeat until FILL hits the target %. Avoid the spinning Qix while drawing. Tip: hold keys (don't tap). On-screen pad works the same.
